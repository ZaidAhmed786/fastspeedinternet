Pure Fiber Optic Connectivity

Fiber utilizes pulses of light traveling through glass strands to provide lightning-fast, symmetrical bandwidth with near-zero latency.

  • Symmetrical Speeds: Upload as fast as you download
  • Peak Reliability: Immune to electrical interference & weather
  • Zero Throttling: True 100% unlimited data caps

High-Speed Cable Broadband

High-capacity hybrid fiber-coaxial networks reaching the vast majority of US households with robust download bandwidth.

  • Broad Footprint: Available in 90%+ of metropolitan areas
  • Fast Setup: Often utilizes existing coaxial wall jacks
  • Bundle Savings: Deep discounts when paired with TV & phone
