Billing & Cancellation Terms

Understanding Billing, Cancellations & Refunds

Effective Date: May 2026

This Refund & Billing Policy applies to the services offered by Fast Speed Internet LLC, doing business as Fast Speed Internet through fastspeedinternet.net and our telephone concierge lines.

1. 100% Free Consumer Plan Advisory Services

Our broadband comparison, speed assessments, address qualification, and order facilitation services are provided to residential and small business consumers completely free of charge. We do not assess or collect consultation fees, membership charges, or broker commissions from the customer. Consequently, there are no consulting fees to be refunded.

2. Telecommunications Carrier Monthly Billing & Invoicing

Fast Speed Internet does not directly bill or collect recurring payments for internet, television, or phone connectivity. All recurring monthly service charges, equipment rental fees, technician installation fees, government taxes, and regulatory recovery surcharges are billed directly by the applicable third-party telecommunications network provider chosen by the consumer.

All billing questions, payment processing, invoice disputes, fee adjustments, and payment method updates must be conducted directly through your carrier's customer billing department.

3. Order Cancellations Prior to Installation

If you place a new internet or bundle order with our concierge and decide to cancel prior to technician installation or equipment activation:

  • You may cancel the pending order at any time before service activation without incurring any cancellation or broker penalty fees from Fast Speed Internet.
  • To cancel a pending order, call our concierge team at +1 (888) 963-5771 or call the carrier directly with your order confirmation number.
  • Any upfront installation deposits or hardware advance payments paid directly to the carrier will be refunded by the carrier according to their respective cancellation terms.
4. Carrier Money-Back Satisfaction Guarantees

Many major broadband providers offer a 14-day or 30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee on new residential internet lines. If you are unsatisfied with your internet connection speed or reliability during the provider's promotional guarantee window:

  • You must contact the carrier directly within their designated guarantee timeframe to terminate service.
  • All carrier-supplied equipment (such as optical network terminals, cable modems, Wi-Fi gateways, or satellite receivers) must be returned in good working condition via prepaid carrier return boxes or authorized drop-off retail locations to avoid unreturned equipment charges.
  • Carrier credits or refunds are issued directly to the customer's original method of payment.
5. Ongoing Service Termination & Equipment Return

If you choose to cancel or modify your active internet subscription after the initial installation and trial window, termination of service is governed by your contract with the carrier. Month-to-month plans can typically be canceled without early termination fees (ETFs), while annual term plans may have specific ETF provisions detailed by the provider. Fast Speed Internet is not responsible for any carrier early termination fees.
