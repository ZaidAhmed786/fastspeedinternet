Consumer Privacy Commitment

How We Protect & Handle Your Information

Effective Date: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fast Speed Internet LLC, doing business as Fast Speed Internet ("we," "our," or "us"), collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information obtained through fastspeedinternet.net, inbound/outbound telephone interactions, and our broadband plan comparison services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide accurate broadband availability checks and assist in plan selection:

  • Contact Identifiers: Name, phone number, email address, and preferred contact time.
  • Location Data: Street address, apartment/unit number, city, state, and 5-digit ZIP code (required to query carrier network coverage databases).
  • Service Requirements: Current internet provider, desired download/upload speeds, bundle preferences (TV/Phone), and business vs. residential status.
  • Automated Device & Browsing Data: IP address, device type, browser characteristics, referring URLs, operating system, and on-site click interactions.
  • Call Recordings: Inbound and outbound phone calls with our concierge may be recorded or monitored for customer quality assurance, compliance verification, and order confirmation.
2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected personal information exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • Qualifying address-level broadband coverage across fiber, cable, 5G wireless, and satellite carrier networks.
  • Delivering personalized speed and pricing comparison recommendations over the phone or via email.
  • Facilitating authorized customer order submissions into telecommunications carrier provisioning systems.
  • Fulfilling customer service inquiries, callback requests, and follow-up support.
  • Complying with regulatory obligations and resolving any service discrepancies.
3. Cookies, Advertising & Conversion Tracking

We use cookies, web beacons, and advertising tracking scripts to evaluate website performance, improve user experience, and measure campaign effectiveness (such as telephone call conversions from our advertisements).

These cookies do not collect sensitive personally identifiable information. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings. You may also opt out of personalized online advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page or the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice tool.

4. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers.

Your information is shared only under strict operational guidelines:

  • Authorized Network Carriers & Master Agents: When you decide to proceed with an order, your contact and address details are submitted directly to the selected service provider and their authorized master distributors (such as DSI Systems) to provision your service, schedule technician installation, and establish your account.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: When required by federal or state law, court order, or governmental subpoena.
5. Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) Consent

By submitting your phone number through our contact or callback forms, or by initiating a call to our toll-free phone number +1 (888) 963-5771, you provide express written consent for Fast Speed Internet (and its authorized agents) to contact you via telephone call or SMS text message regarding your broadband inquiry, even if your phone number is currently listed on any internal, corporate, state, or federal Do-Not-Call (DNC) registry. Consent is not a mandatory condition of purchase; you may revoke consent at any time by notifying our support team.

6. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA & State Protections)

Depending on your state of residence (including California, Virginia, Colorado, and others), you have specific statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: You can request a summary of the personal information categories we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You can request the deletion of your personal data from our operational databases.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge different prices, or provide a lower quality of service for exercising your privacy rights.

To submit a privacy rights request, please contact our privacy compliance officer at support@fastspeedinternet.net with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request".

7. Information Security

We employ industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical safeguards—including SSL/TLS data encryption in transit and restricted database access—to protect your personal information against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
