Who We Are

Your Trusted Telecommunications Concierge

Fast Speed Internet is a registered trade name (DBA) owned and operated by Fast Speed Internet LLC, a registered corporate entity headquartered in Carlsbad, California.

We operate as an independent broadband sales agency, marketing partner, and consumer plan concierge. We are not an Internet Service Provider (ISP), and we do not maintain or operate physical telecom infrastructure. Instead, our mission is to simplify the often frustrating process of shopping for residential and business connectivity.

Rather than spending hours calling different cable and telecom utilities, customers turn to Fast Speed Internet as a single point of contact. We run instant footprint checks across national databases, evaluate available fiber optic, cable broadband, 5G wireless, and satellite options, and assist you with transparent plan selection and order fulfillment.

"Our goal is straightforward: provide transparent, unbiased broadband comparisons and white-glove ordering assistance so every customer receives the fastest speeds and lowest rates available at their address."

Consumer Advocacy

Unlike individual internet utilities who only sell their own proprietary service, we evaluate all network carriers reaching your address. We recommend plans that honestly fit your speed demands and budget.

Authorized Distribution

We work with established telecommunications distributors and master agents to access authorized carrier provisioning channels, ensuring your orders, promotional rates, and installation dates are booked accurately.

Strict Policy Compliance

We are committed to full compliance with Google Ads advertising guidelines, FTC consumer protection rules, and carrier brand standards. We maintain 100% transparency about our independent agency status.
